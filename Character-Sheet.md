# Character sheet

## Backgrounds

At the Imbuing a hunter gets the book's five dots of Background, spread at random over its nine.
Only the three that change play are kept, so most hunters have one or two dots between them.

| Background | What it does |
|---|---|
| Destiny | Once a day for each dot, a botched Edge roll is rolled again |
| Arsenal | 3% better prices a dot when this hunter trades with a [hunter cell's](Hunter-Net.md) post. A hunter who starts with it brings a hunter weapon a dot: a stake crossbow, a fire lance, an aspergillum, an incendiary shotgun |
| Mentor | An older hunter visits now and then with a monster's weakness or the lair of a monster the colony knows. More dots, more visits |

## Awareness

The feel for when something isn't human. A hunter gains a dot for every three monsters they've come
to know, up to 5. Each dot is a die more when second sight meets a monster too strong for it.

## Attributes and Willpower

A hunter has the same nine Attributes as everyone in The Rim by Night, read from their skills and
health. Experience raises them: a dot costs four times the current rating, up to 5. Each dot past an
ordinary person's gives the same small bonuses it gives Kindred and Garou (melee damage for
Strength, dodge for Dexterity, and so on).

Willpower is the ordinary kind. It pays for mundane feats, never for Edges, and a dot costs its
current rating.

## Experience

A hunter earns 0.3 experience a day, plus some for each kill, twice as much for a monster. The
Experience gained setting scales it. Virtues, Conviction and Edges never come from experience: those
come from deeds and cashing in Conviction.
