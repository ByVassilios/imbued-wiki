# Living with monsters

Vampire and Garou colonists are allowed beside hunters. A hunter who comes to know a colonist is a
monster knows it for good, and how they live with it depends on the Creed. There's no mood penalty,
and it doesn't fade.

## How a hunter comes to know

- Second sight marks the colonist while it's up for another reason.
- The hunter sees the colonist feed, take a shape no person has, or use a power.
- Discern, Witness or a named suspect shows it.

The Imbued tab lists every monster a hunter knows.

## By Creed

| Creed | Toward a known monster colonist |
|---|---|
| Avenger | Denounces it and fights it more often. Rarely, a righteous fury: they go for the monster with whatever is at hand, three times as likely for a day after seeing a monster feed or hurt someone |
| Defender | Squares up to it if it hurts one of the colony in sight |
| Judge | Argues about it, and weighs it in talks with other colonists, who come away thinking better or worse of it |
| Innocent | Talks to it without fear |
| Martyr | Denounces it, and squares up to it if it feeds on anyone in sight |
| Redeemer | Reminds it of what it was. A vampire may win back a dot of Humanity, a Garou loses a point of Rage. It can start a fight |
| Visionary | Asks it questions |

## With Kindred loaded

- Vampires are monsters to second sight, masked or not. Elders of generation 8 or lower are hard to
  see; generation 5 and older are harder.
- Expose breaks the Masquerade for everyone who sees it.
- Fang marks on a drained body are clues.

## With Garou loaded

- Garou are monsters in every form, Homid too. Rank 4 and 5 are hard to see.
- Expose breaches the Veil. Becalm can end a frenzy.
- Suspend seals the map: no stepping sideways, no spirits called up, no shadow crossings, for an hour.
- Claw marks and a fomor's taint are clues, and a fomor's lead is a Pentex subsidiary.
