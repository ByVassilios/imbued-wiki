# Captured monsters and the board

A monster in your prison is someone a hunter can talk to: about who it was, what it wants, what hurts it, who
else is out there, and whether it could be something better. The other cells talk too, on notes left on the
[case board](Hunter-Tools.md#the-case-board).

## Talking to a captured monster

Only a hunter can talk to one, and only a prisoner the colony knows for a monster. One that still looks mortal
has to be found out first (second sight, a clue, a dissection). Shamblers have nothing to say.

**Talk to (name)** is on the right-click menu. The hunter walks over, the talk window opens with the monster's file
beside it, and everything it says is written into the file under **What it told us**. A monster that's asleep,
raging or out of patience says so instead.

| Topic | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Who it was | Its life before. Its clan or tribe shows in about a third of the answers |
| What it wants | What it's after, in its own words |
| What it is | Its kind's weakness. The first true answer adds half an autopsy's worth to the colony's file and counts as a Visionary's deed |
| Others | The first thing it can give up: the culprit of a town case you're working, a lair (you get the site), or a vampire court or Garou sept near the colony |
| Redemption | See below |
| Its word | It swears to leave the colony alone if let go. It can only swear once |

The tones are the ones for [talking to townsfolk](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#talking): Kindly (Charisma +
Social at 6), Firmly (Manipulation + Social at 6), Threaten (Strength + Social at 7, two patience). An Innocent
rolls two dice more. A failed question gets a refusal, and a botched threat ends the talk for the day. Its
patience is the same 2 to 4 a day as anyone's.

### Lies

About three times in ten (four in ten when threatened) a monster lies about who it was, its weakness, or the
others, half as often once it's past half redeemed. A lie gives nothing. A hunter with the Pierce Edge always catches it, and a Judge catches it on
Perception + their first Virtue at 7. A caught lie is marked in the file.

### It talks back

After every question the monster rolls its Manipulation + 2 against the hunter's Willpower, both at 6. If it
wins, it says something that gets under the hunter's skin:

- **Doubt.** The hunter loses a point of Conviction. Not an Innocent.
- **Its words linger.** A mood hit for a few days, twice as long for an Innocent.
- **Anger.** Now and then the hunter's next question comes out as a threat whatever they picked.

### Redemption

Asking about redemption is a roll like any other, once a day per monster, and fills a pool to 100.

- **Kindly:** 4 points per success.
- **Firmly or Threaten:** 7 points per success, but each talk hurts it and it takes it badly.
- **A Redeemer** fills it twice as fast. An Avenger who tries takes -3 mood for 3 days for having talked to it like a person.
- **Beyond redemption:** a vampire below Humanity 4 on Humanity can't be reached. One on a Path still can.

At 100 it joins the colony. Every Mercy hunter in the colony gains a point of Conviction, and the one who did it
gets a Redeemer's deed. One broken by force rather than talked round remembers it.

- **A revenant** is laid to rest instead, and the colony is glad of it.
- **A necromancer** lets its dead go and gives up the power.
- **A traitor.** A vampire or Garou that gave up others counts as one to its own. If its old faction is hostile,
  it raids the colony 10 to 20 days later to get it back.

### Letting it go on its word

Once it has sworn, **Let it go on the word given** sets it free. Whether it keeps its word is rolled then:
30%, plus 5% for every kindly answer it gave (less for every threat), plus 20% if it's past half redeemed, at
most 80%.

- **Kept:** once within a year, it sends you a letter warning of an attack by its own kind.
- **Broken:** it comes back 5 to 15 days later, with others, as a [first horror](Hunter-Tools.md#the-first-horror)
  does.

A vampire or Garou let go after giving up others has left its sect or sept by the time it's off the map.

## The hunters' board

A case board also takes the other cells' notes. Nobody sees them come: one is pinned in the night every 1 to 3
days, and sooner after something the cells would talk about. Only the board shows them. Its inspect line counts
the notes nobody has read, and they stay sealed until someone does.

- **Read the board** (the board's button) opens the notes window, with the clippings of solved cases on a second tab.
- **Send someone to read** (in the notes window) sends the nearest free colonist over. Colonists also read it in their free time, as
  recreation. Reading takes about an hour, and good or bad news in the notes shows in their mood.

Each note is signed with a handle. Hover over it to see the hunter's real name and cell. Up to 40 notes stay up;
older ones come down.

| Notes | What they are |
|---|---|
| Cells | A cell's own life, a cell under attack, a hunter killed, and the warning after a trap |
| Deeds | Talk of the colony's own work: a case solved, a monster killed, redeemed, let go or dissected |
| World | Word of the world, the same rumors a caravan brings |
| Leads | A lair or a town with something wrong in it. Follow it for the site, or test it first |
| Talk | Arguments between the Creeds, at most one note in five |
| Everyday | The weather, lost knives, a fair in town. Now and then one ends with a lead |
| Yours | The colony's own notes |

Notes about deeds and arguments get 2 to 4 replies from other hunters, each in their Creed's voice.

### Leads and bait

About one lead in eight was pinned by monsters who read the board. It's written the same way, but more of them
wait at the site than it says, and afterwards a note warns the cells about the handle that wrote it.

**Test it for bait** before following a lead: a hunter with Pierce always tells, and a Judge rolls Perception + their
first Virtue at 7 and on a failure can't tell. The result is written on the note.

### Your own notes

**Pin a note** puts up one of the colony's own, each kind once a week:

| Note | What it does |
|---|---|
| Share a finding | Needs a file from the autopsy table or something a monster told you. The cells' goodwill goes up by 5, and they reply |
| Ask for help | A few of the cells' hunters walk in to fight beside you, as an ally's military aid. Needs the cells friendly. Works without a comms console |
| Warn of one that got away | Needs a monster you let go or one that escaped. Half the time a cell hunts it down and says so on the board |
