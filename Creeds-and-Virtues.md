# Creeds and Virtues

## The seven Creeds

| Creed | Virtue | Conviction | Path | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Avenger** | Zeal | 4 | Vengeance | The monsters have to be destroyed, every one of them, and the Avenger means to do it. Of all the hunters they are the readiest to fight and the least willing to stop. |
| **Defender** | Zeal | 3 | Defense | Someone has to stand between the monsters and the people they prey on. The Defender guards a home, a family or a whole colony, and fights to keep it safe. |
| **Judge** | Zeal | 3 | Judgment | Not every monster is the same, and not every one deserves the same end. The Judge weighs what a creature has done before deciding what to do about it, then acts without flinching. |
| **Innocent** | Mercy | 3 | Innocence | The Innocent sees the monsters as they are and isn't afraid of them. They ask questions where others would shoot, and trust that understanding can do more than a weapon. |
| **Martyr** | Mercy | 4 | Martyrdom | Whatever it costs, the Martyr pays it so that no one else has to. They take the blow meant for someone else and count their own pain as the price of saving others. |
| **Redeemer** | Mercy | 3 | Redemption | Something human may still be left inside a monster. The Redeemer looks for it, and tries to bring it back before deciding the creature is beyond saving. |
| **Visionary** | Vision | 3 | Vision | Why were they chosen, and what for? The Visionary wants to know what the monsters are, what the Imbuing means and where it all leads, and looks for the answers before anything else. |

Within the Virtue the act picked, the Creed leans by what the colonist is like:

| Virtue | Creed | Leans to it |
|---|---|---|
| Zeal | Avenger | Bloodlust, a Melee passion |
| Zeal | Defender | Kind, or a loved one hurt by the monster |
| Zeal | Judge | An Intellectual or Social passion |
| Mercy | Innocent | Young adults, under 21 |
| Mercy | Martyr | Masochist, or a loved one killed |
| Mercy | Redeemer | A Medicine passion |
| Vision | Visionary | Everyone |

## Virtues

Mercy, Vision and Zeal run from 0 to 10. The Creed's own Virtue, the primary, is never passed by the
other two. Virtues rise only one way: cashing in 10 Conviction. A letter lists the Virtues that may
rise and the Edges each would open, and the hunter drops to 0 Conviction after.

## Deeds

Each deed gives 1 Conviction, once a day for each kind.

| Creed | The deed |
|---|---|
| Avenger | Kills a monster, or fights three at once and lives |
| Defender | Carries a colonist a monster hurt to safety |
| Judge | Shows a disguised monster for what it is |
| Innocent | Talks to a monster and walks away |
| Martyr | Gets hurt by a monster standing over a downed friend |
| Redeemer | Heals someone a monster hurt |
| Visionary | Learns a weakness, a past or a lair |

Any hunter also gains a point for killing a monster with fire, a trap or sunlight, and for winning
an argument with a hunter of another Creed.

## Betrayals

Acting against the Creed costs a point: an Avenger fleeing a fight they could take, a Defender leaving
a downed colonist to a monster, a Redeemer executing a prisoner, a Visionary striking first at a
monster they know nothing about, an Innocent standing by while a monster feeds. Judges and Martyrs
have none.

## Derangements

At 7 in any Virtue, and at each point after, the hunter gains a derangement that fits it: Zeal leans to
paranoia, obsession and mania, Mercy to multiple personalities and hysteria, Vision to schizophrenia
and obsession. Seeing a loved one or another hunter killed by a monster calls for a Willpower roll at 7,
and a failure brings one too. Core's settings can turn derangements off.
