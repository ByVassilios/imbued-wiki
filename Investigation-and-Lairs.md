# Investigation and lairs

A monster among your visitors or colonists leaves marks. Hunters read them, work out who it is, and
then go and find where it sleeps.

## Clues

A monster that isn't the colony's open enemy leaves clues on a home map. Each one fades in 15 days.

| Clue | Where it's left | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Fang marks | A body a vampire drank dry (Kindred) | 3 |
| Claw marks | A body a Garou killed (Garou) | 3 |
| Disturbed ground | Where a dead one got up on a map a revenant haunts | 2 |
| Tracks that stop | Where a power carried a monster more than 6 cells at once, or off the map | 2 |
| Strange blood | Where a monster was hurt, one every four hours | 1 |
| Witness's account | Where an outsider saw a monster break the Masquerade or the Veil (Kindred or Garou) | 2 |
| A fomor's taint | Where a fomor used a power (Garou) | 2 |

Every clue names the monster that left it and up to five others within 15 cells as suspects.

## Examining

Examining a clue is research work, for hunters and Bystanders only; to anyone else it's a stain. It
takes ten seconds and rolls Perception + Intellectual at 6, and the clue is used up. Each suspect gets
a point; the one who left it gets the clue's weight plus a point a success. A botch puts the weight on
the wrong person. A hunter with Discern or Witness up also reads the kind of creature.

Some Edges add more: Witness shows past meetings of monsters in sight, Pierce's touch tells a
suspect's word true or false, and Delve reads every clue within 8 cells at once.

## The suspect list

The **Suspects** link at the foot of the Imbued tab opens a list of everyone with evidence against
them, and every monster a colony hunter knows that isn't an enemy. At six points, or when a hunter
already knows it, the tab's hunter can name a suspect on the same map.

- **Right:** everyone who can see the monster sees it for what it is, every colony hunter knows it,
  and a visitor goes berserk or runs. A Judge gains a point for it.
- **Wrong:** the innocent thinks much less of the hunter for 30 days, their faction loses 10
  goodwill, every colonist is uneasy for three days, and a Judge loses a point.

## Leads and lairs

A lead opens a lair on the world map 2 to 8 tiles away, as a quest in the Quests tab that ends when the
lair is cleared:

- A monster named while visiting gives its lead when it leaves the map.
- A witness who got away with what a visiting monster did tells the Courier Union, and that monster gives its
  lead when it leaves. A colonist's breach never does.
- A monster a colony hunter is trailing gives it as it goes.
- Pinpoint at three successes gives the lair of the monster's kind.
- A [mentor](Hunter-Net.md#mentors) can bring one.

| Lair | Whose |
|---|---|
| Haven | A vampire's (Kindred) |
| Den | A Black Spiral Dancer's, with the Wyrm (Garou) |
| Pentex subsidiary | A fomor's (Garou) |
| Grave | The hidden and shamblers |
| Necromancer's house | The necromancer and the dead |
| Revenant's old home | A revenant |

Send a caravan. The named or trailed monster waits there if it's still alive. Once no enemy is left
standing, every colony hunter there gains a point of Conviction, and an afflicted lair keeps shambler
raids and revenant visits away for a season. Each lair also holds a hunter weapon near the middle.
