# Investigation and lairs

A monster among your visitors or colonists leaves marks. Hunters read them, work out who it is, and
then go and find where it sleeps.

## Clues

A monster that isn't the colony's open enemy leaves clues on a home map, or in a town the colony has a case in. Each
one fades in 15 days. A monster the colony's hunters already know leaves none, and neither does a kill in a fight
against the colony's enemies.

| Clue | Where it's left | Read well, it gives |
|---|---|---|
| Fang marks | A body a vampire drank dry (Kindred) | Who was near, and the kind |
| Claw marks | A body a Garou killed (Garou) | Who was near, and the kind |
| Disturbed ground | Where a dead one got up on a map a revenant haunts | Who was near, and the kind |
| A fomor's taint | Where a fomor used a power (Garou) | Who was near, and the kind |
| Witness's account | Where an outsider saw a monster break the Masquerade or the Veil (Kindred or Garou) | Who was near |
| Tracks that stop | Where a power carried a monster more than 6 cells at once, or off the map | A description |
| Strange blood | Where a monster was hurt, one every four hours | A description |
| Tracks | Round a prowling visitor's target's bed, on its first night | Who was near, as two sources |
| Torn carcass | Where a prowling visitor killed an animal, on its second night | Who was near as two sources, and the kind |
| Hidden things | In a culprit's belongings, when a hunter searches them (the hunter walks to the person's bed, or to them if they have none) | The culprit as two sources, and the kind |

A clue remembers the monster that left it and up to five others within 15 cells.

## Examining

Examining a clue is research work, for hunters and Bystanders only; to anyone else it's a stain. It
takes ten seconds and rolls Perception + Intellectual at 6, and the clue is used up. Nothing is added up for you:
the reading goes in the case window and you piece it together.

- **Who was near.** The reading lists everyone who was close, in no order. Each success rules out one innocent, but it
  always leaves at least one beside the one who did it.
- **A description.** Strange blood and tracks that stop name nobody. Each success gives a piece of what whoever left
  it looks like: a man or a woman, young, middle-aged or old, thin, heavy, big or ordinary, the colour of their hair,
  what they wear and its colour, a scar or a missing part, the weapon they carry. Each piece goes on the case, and a
  suspect's file ticks or crosses it against how they look now.
- **The kind.** A kind's own mark read well shows what did it, and so does a hunter with Discern or Witness up.
- **A botch** looks like any poor reading, but it rules out the one who did it.

Some Edges add more: Witness shows past meetings of monsters in sight, Pierce's touch tells a
suspect's word true or false, and Delve reads every clue within 8 cells at once. Pierce and the autopsy table count
as two sources against a monster and show its kind.

## Prowling visitors

A revenant or a hidden one doesn't come alone. It arrives with two or three ordinary visitors of the same faction,
and they all stay a few days. On its first night it leaves tracks round its target's bed. On its second it kills a
farm animal, or a colonist's pet if there's no livestock, and leaves the carcass. On its third it attacks its target.
A body that gets up on those nights leaves disturbed ground too.

Read the clues and name it before the third night, and it fights or runs. Otherwise the fight is at the
target's bedside. Name it on too little and nobody believes it: it strikes the next night.

## Town cases

While the colony has a hunter, word comes now and then of an attack in an ordinary town within 12 tiles (or 12 tiles past the nearest one, if none is that close): a letter
and a quest that name the town and say what was found, never what did it. Each case has ten days. No new rumour
comes while a case is taken, and each one still waiting makes the next half as likely. Rumours are in the Hunters
group on the settings page, with their own on/off and frequency. About one rumour in four comes from a monster that
got away from an earlier case, and the case window says so.

Send a caravan and walk in as guests. Three or four clues lie about the town. After the first, about one in four is
a red herring: blood from a bar fight, a dog's tracks, a drunk's story. Two successes see it for nothing; a worse
reading takes it at its word. Leave and come back, and the unread ones are still there.

Every town the colony visits keeps its people, case or not: whoever you met there is there again next time, unless
they died or left.

Right-click a townsperson to work on them:

| Action | Roll | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| Talk to | See [Talking](#talking) | What they know, as long as their patience lasts |
| Go through their things | Dexterity + Intellectual at 7, once a person | The culprit's hold hidden things to examine. An innocent's are clean. A botch is seen, and the town shuts the case for three days |
| Watch them through the night | None. Start between 18:00 and 2:00, it lasts until 6:00 | The culprit is caught in the act and named. Anyone else slept. Broken off, it tells nothing |

### Talking

**Talk to** (on the right-click menu, or in a suspect's file) sends the hunter over. If the person won't talk, they
say why. If they will, the two stand facing each other, and their file opens with the talk window beside it. Close
the talk window to let them go.

Pick what to ask about, then how to put it:

| Topic | What it gives |
|---|---|
| The attack | Someone who saw it names the culprit (two sources) and what it is. Someone who heard of it gives pieces of a description. A botch, or the culprit itself, names someone innocent |
| Where were you last night | An alibi: a place, and usually someone in town who can say so. The culprit always lies about it, and about one innocent in four lies too (an affair, a theft, a debt), the same ones every time |
| Someone | Pick a person. If they're the one an alibi named, they confirm it or break it, and a broken alibi counts as a source. Otherwise they say they know them or don't, and now and then that they saw them out that night |
| A clue | Pick a clue you've read. The culprit, on a success, slips: it shows what it is, or places itself once more, and it always learns the hunters are onto it. An innocent the clue listed, or whose alibi broke, explains it away and is ruled out, for 3 of the town's goodwill |

| Tone | Roll | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Kindly | Charisma + Social at 6 | None |
| Firmly | Manipulation + Social at 6 | None |
| Threaten | Strength + Social at 7 | Costs two patience. A failure makes the whole town warier (+1 to every roll, up to +2). A botch shuts them for good |
| Bribe | None, 40 silver from the hunters' pockets | Always talks, but lies three times in ten |

Each person has 2 to 4 patience a day, always the same for the same person. A question costs one, a threat two.
When it runs out they say so and walk off; ask again tomorrow. What they said stays in the talk window under **Said
so far**, and in the case's answers.

### Travellers

Visitors and traders passing through a case's town can be suspects, and about one case in seven the culprit is one
of them. Travellers leave 1 to 3 days after the colony arrives, or at once when the culprit among them is warned. A
suspect's file shows when they're due to go. Track one who left (Perception + Animals at 6, from where they walked
off) to find the town they went to. If it was the culprit, the case moves there.

The culprit isn't always local:

- About four times in ten it lives in the town.
- About one time in seven it's a traveller passing through (see above).
- About one time in four it lives in another town 2 to 8 tiles on. Three sources against it name that town, and
  the case moves there with one or two more clues.
- The rest of the time it has a lair. Three sources against it open a lead to it, and it waits there.

The culprit learns the hunters are onto it when it's questioned itself, when three sources place it, or when it's named
and not believed. Half a day later, while the colony is there, it kills someone who answered the hunters and leaves its
mark on the body, or it goes to its lair and leaves a lead.

| Name | What happens |
|---|---|
| Right, believed | As at home, and the case is solved (see [Solving a case](#solving-a-case)). The body can go home for the autopsy table |
| Right, not believed | Nothing, and the culprit knows now. It can't be named again until a new source places it |
| Wrong | As at home, and the town wants the hunters gone: for three days nobody there can read a clue, ask a question or name anyone, and the deadline keeps running |
| Killed without naming | The usual penalties for attacking your hosts |
| It runs off the map | A lead to its lair |

Killing the culprit at its lair solves the case too.

### Solving a case

| Reward | How much |
|---|---|
| Silver | 200, plus 100 for each red herring, 200 if the case moved to a third town and 300 for a lair. Half as much again if solved within half the deadline |
| Goodwill | The town's faction: 10, plus 5 a point of the same, up to 30 |
| Hunter-Net standing | 10 to 25, by the same points (see [Standing](Hunter-Net.md#standing)) |
| Conviction | A point to every hunter there |
| Skill | 800 Intellectual and 800 Social to every colonist who worked the case: read its clues, talked to its people, searched or watched them, or named it |
| A keepsake | Two cases in five: something of the culprit's (a tooth, a ring, a claw, a locket, a key, a hat), worth looking at |
| A clipping | Pinned up on a [Hunters' Board](Hunter-Tools.md#the-hunters-board), if the colony has one |

Miss the deadline and a second rumour comes from the same town, with more dead and ten more days. Miss that one
too and the trail goes cold. A few days later the monster turns up at the colony among visitors, prowling.

## The case window

The **Cases** page of the Hunters tab (also the **Cases** link at the foot of the Imbued tab) is the case window. Open cases are on the left with their days
left, then **Others**: everyone with evidence who isn't in a case, and every monster a colony hunter knows that isn't
an enemy. Solved and cold cases sit below. The right side shows the chosen case: where it is, how long is left, what
did it once something showed the kind, and how many clues are read, then three tabs:

- **Suspects**: everyone the case has turned up, in three groups one under the other: still in it, ruled out by a
  reading, and crossed off by you. Hover a name for how they look and what placed them; click it for their file: their portrait, age and
  where they live (or who they travel with, and when they leave), then **Looks** (how they look now beside each
  thing said of the culprit, with a tick or a cross), **Alibi** (where they say they were, and whether it held) and
  **Evidence** (what placed them, what ruled them out, how many sources, whether they were searched or have left).
  **Actions** (on the row and in the file) are talk, track, name, and cross off or put back; crossing off is only for
  your notes. A greyed name says why underneath.
  **Track** finds them: on a map, at a lair a lead names, or in their town. Someone who walks off a map while a
  suspect leaves footsteps for two days. The tab's hunter, standing where they left, gets one try to follow them
  (Perception + Animals at 6): a monster's lead to its lair, anyone else's to the nearest town of their own.
- **Clues**: a card a clue, two across, with who read it and how well, the people near it as tags (struck through
  where ruled out) or the description it gave. A tag in the corner says what it showed. Unread ones have a link to
  where they lie.
- **Answers**: a transcript: who said it and which hunter asked, searched or watched them, then what they said or
  what the hunter found.

At 60 Hunter-Net standing a live town case has a **Call a cell** link: two of a cell's hunters come to the case's
town or lair and fight beside the colony's people there, once every ten days.

Clues read at home go in a home case, which goes cold 15 days after its last clue.

## Naming

The tab's hunter can name a suspect on the same map once something showed what kind of creature did it and two
sources place them there, or when a colony hunter already knows them. Being nameable doesn't make someone guilty.

A right name is believed about two times in five with two sources, seven in ten with three, and always with four or
more, or when a hunter knows the monster. Caught in a stakeout, it's always believed.

- **Right:** everyone who can see the monster sees it for what it is, every colony hunter knows it,
  and a visitor fights as any enemy of ours does or runs. A Judge gains a point for it.
- **Wrong:** the innocent thinks much less of the hunter for 30 days, their faction loses 10
  goodwill, every colonist is uneasy for three days, and a Judge loses a point.

Showing a monster for what it is, by naming it or with Expose, depends on who it lives among:

| Where | What its people do |
|---|---|
| A settlement of its own kind (a Kindred court, a sept, a Pentex front) | The settlement turns hostile at once |
| An ordinary town | It's nobody's to defend, so killing it costs nothing. Half the time the other monsters hiding there turn on the hunters |
| Visiting the colony, from a faction of its own kind | That faction loses 25 goodwill |
| Visiting the colony, from an ordinary faction | It's nobody's to defend |

Witnesses who saw a monster that isn't the colony's don't count against the Masquerade or the Veil.

## Leads and lairs

A lead opens a lair on the world map 2 to 8 tiles away, as a quest in the Quests tab that ends when the
lair is cleared:

- A monster named while visiting gives its lead when it leaves the map.
- A town case whose culprit has a lair gives it once there's enough evidence.
- A witness who got away with what a visiting monster did tells the other cells, and that monster gives its
  lead when it leaves. A colonist's breach never does.
- A monster a colony hunter is trailing gives it as it goes.
- Pinpoint at three successes gives the lair of the monster's kind.
- A [mentor](Hunter-Net.md#mentors) can bring one.

| Lair | Whose |
|---|---|
| Haven | A vampire's (Kindred) |
| Den | A Black Spiral Dancer's, with the Wyrm (Garou) |
| Pentex subsidiary | A fomor's (Garou) |
| Grave | The hidden and shamblers |
| Necromancer's house | The necromancer and the dead |
| Revenant's old home | A revenant |

Send a caravan. The named or trailed monster waits there if it's still alive. Once no enemy is left
standing, every colony hunter there gains a point of Conviction, and an afflicted lair keeps shambler
raids and revenant visits away for a season. Each lair also holds a hunter weapon near the middle.
