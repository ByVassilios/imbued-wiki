# Being imbued

An imbued hunter is still human. They don't heal faster, they don't frenzy, and a bullet hurts
them like anyone else. What they have is sight, Conviction, Edges and each other. They also stand and fight where another colonist would run, until they're below half health or hiding. The Ignore response still holds them back.

## The Imbuing

A colonist who sees a monster hurt someone (a vampire feeding, a Garou's blow, a afflicted
attacking) rolls for the Imbuing. The base chance is 2%. Brave, Kind or Tough colonists roll twice
as often, Wimps half as often, and nobody in a mental break is chosen. Each monster can imbue at most
one colonist a day. A colony with more than three hunters gets at most one new one a season.

What the colonist does in the next three seconds picks the Virtue:

| They... | Virtue |
|---|---|
| Attack the monster | Zeal |
| Rescue, tend or carry someone hurt, or capture the monster | Mercy |
| Anything else: run, hide, watch | Vision |

Traits and passions then pick the Creed within that Virtue; see
[Creeds and Virtues](Creeds-and-Virtues.md). A letter from the Messengers names it. The new hunter
starts with two points in the Creed's Virtue and one more rolled among the three, Conviction at the
Creed's level, and the first Edge of the Creed's path. Every hunter also holds True Faith 5, the most
there is: vampires fear it, and it adds to Willpower.

## The hunted stranger

A colony with no hunters gets a visitor by night some time between day 15 and day 40: a stranger
who has just been Imbued, running from the monster that woke them. They come as an offer in the
Quests tab, open for two days. Let it run out, and they leave and take the monster with them. Accept it, and they join as a hunter
with a Creed of their own (more often a Vision Creed, as they ran) and its first Edge. Two to
five hours later, once it is dark, the monster comes after them alone: a shambler, or with Kindred
or Garou loaded a young vampire or a fomor. It is the new hunter's first horror; see
[Hunter tools](Hunter-Tools.md#the-first-horror).

Turned away, the stranger comes again 30 to 60 days later, until the colony has a hunter
of its own. The Imbued settings can turn this off.

## Bystanders

A colonist who misses the roll by a little becomes a Bystander. They get no sight and no Edges, but
they know monsters exist: they panic a little less, and they work 10% faster at research and tending.

## Conviction

Conviction (1 to 10) is the hunter's certainty that they're doing right. It pays for second sight
and for most Edges above level 3. It comes back after each threat ends, up to the Creed's level, and
deeds raise it past that. At 0 the hunter is an ordinary person to every monster, and a mood thought
says so. At 10 the hunter can cash it in for a Virtue point, which leaves them at 3.

## Second sight

When a hostile monster comes within 15 cells and in view, or aims a power at the hunter, the hunter
spends 1 Conviction at once. For three hours:

- Every monster they see gets a mark over it. The mark says "not human", not what kind.
- Their mind is closed: no Dominate, no Presence, no Delirium, no possession.
- Each monster seen this way is known to the hunter from then on (the Imbued tab lists them).
- Illusions they see are marked "false". They won't aim at one, and one blow from them ends it. A
  vampire's phantom beast gets no mark, but a blow ends it too.
- Smoke no longer spoils their aim, though they won't pick a new target through it on their own.

Some monsters are too strong to see: an elder vampire, a Garou of rank 4 or more, a necromancer. The
hunter rolls their primary Virtue plus Awareness at 6 (8 against the very oldest). On a failure that
one monster stays hidden from them for the three hours, and its powers can still reach their mind.

## The cell

Every hunter in the colony is in its cell. Cell members think a little better of each other, and
when one hunter's sight wakes, the others within 10 cells who can see them spend their own point at
once instead of waiting to see the monster themselves.
