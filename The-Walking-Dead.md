# The afflicted

The mod's own monsters. They come with or without Kindred and Garou.

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| Shambler | Slow, strong and hard to stop. Feels no pain and doesn't go down to wounds; only the head or the whole body ends it. Comes in groups, at night |
| Revenant | Someone back from the dead for one person. Passes for living at a distance and mends a wound for each point of Willpower. Once its target is dead, it falls for good |
| Hidden | Passes for living and keeps its skills. Kills for one purpose. The toughest of the three |
| Necromancer | A living sorcerer. Raises a corpse within 10 cells every 10 seconds, up to six at once. When the necromancer dies, those dead fall |

## Where they come from

- **Shambler raids.** The dead dig out of the ground at the map edge at night. In a colony with no
  vampire or Garou to draw other enemies they come half again as often. One raid in six has a
  necromancer walking behind it.
- **Revenant visits.** A revenant or a hidden arrives alone as an ordinary visitor of a friendly
  faction, back for one colonist. Someone who has killed is likelier to be the one. It strikes at
  night. The map stays haunted while it's there.
- **The colony's own dead.** On a haunted map, unburied corpses can get up as shamblers, a fifth of
  them each night hour. Anything in a grave or a sarcophagus stays down.

## Fighting them

Fire does half again as much to them, and the head ends them fastest. Shamblers are slow with doors;
revenants aren't. Ordinary people who see one of the afflicted for what it is get a mood shock, and family
and friends of one who rose take it harder.

## Putting one to rest

A revenant or a hidden that a colony hunter has seen, and that hasn't struck yet, can be talked down
by its target, a Redeemer or an Innocent: about twenty minutes, then Social + Mercy at 7, two dice
more for the one it came back for. A failure and it strikes at once.
