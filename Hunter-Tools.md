# The table, the horror and the last stand

Hunters are mortals, so most of this rewards getting ready before the fight. All of it works without
Kindred or Garou.

## The autopsy table

Build it from the Misc tab. Then lay a subject on it: a downed monster, a staked vampire, any downed
person, any prisoner, a person's corpse, or a piece of a first horror. Animals, insects and machines don't count. Right-click the subject and pick **Carry to the
autopsy table** (anyone can do this), or use vanilla's Carry while drafted. Straps stop a downed subject
crawling off and hold a prisoner down until someone carries them away. Nobody sleeps on the table.

Select the table and pick **Study subject**. A hunter or Bystander on research work does the rest.

| Procedure | Time | Roll | Gives | To the subject |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Examine | 15 minutes | Perception + Intellectual at 6 | Monster or person, the kind on 2 successes, half a point | Nothing |
| Test | 30 minutes, uses up one silver, chemfuel or holy water | Perception + Intellectual at 7 | The kind's weakness on 2 successes, 1 point | One wound of the item's damage |
| Dissect | 3 rolls, an hour each | Stamina + Intellectual at 7, added up | Up to 2 points, and proof for a case | See below |

Holy water comes in flasks: three from 20 silver and 10 steel at a smithy, once Smithing is researched, or bought from a trader who stocks the arsenal. The item must be on the map and not forbidden.

A failed Examine says nothing. A botch reads a person as a monster or a monster as a person.

| Dissection total | Corpse | Living or staked |
|---|---|---|
| Any roll botches | Ruined, no points | Wakes if it can; a stake slips |
| 0 to 2 | Ruined, 1 point | Dies, 1 point |
| 3 to 5 | Used up, 1 point | Dies, 2 points |
| 6 or more | Used up, 2 points | Lives, badly wounded and missing a part, 2 points |

### Waking up

Before each procedure, a subject may get up:

- Staked: never, unless a botch slips the stake.
- In torpor with no stake: 10% for Examine, 25% for Test, 50% for each Dissect roll.
- A downed Garou: the same, doubled while it mends.
- A dead one's corpse: 10% a procedure while a necromancer or revenant is on the map.

### Files

The colony keeps one file for each kind of monster, and one subject gives at most 2 points ever.

| Points | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1 | The file shows on the Imbued tab |
| 3 | Every colonist's blows on that kind do 10% more |
| 6 | 25% more |

Two subjects of one clan or tribe add 10% against that clan or tribe. Nothing adds up past 25%,
counting Pinpoint. A fomor has its own file. A ghoul gives half a point to the vampire file.

### The notes

The first study writes a book of hunter's notes beside the table. A hunter or Bystander can right-click it to
write a copy, which holds what was known that day. The bonuses use the most complete copy on a home map. Lose
every copy and the files go to zero, and subjects you've used stay used, so keep a copy somewhere safe.

### Cutting up a person

A person gives no points. Examining one costs nothing.

- A corpse: only the one cutting minds, at -10 mood for 30 days. A hunter doesn't mind unless their
  beliefs forbid it. Psychopaths don't mind.
- Someone alive: they die, the one cutting takes -25 mood for 60 days, the colony reacts as to an organ
  harvest, and a hunter loses 2 Conviction. Their faction never learns of it.

A dissection is also proof: a monster's case gains the successes and its kind, and an innocent's closes.
Any finding counts as a Visionary's deed. A Redeemer who dissects a living monster pays as for an execution.

## The case board

A cork board of clippings, built from 20 wood (Misc). Each solved case adds one, and hovering over it lists them:
when, who and where. Colonists who see it feel better the more cases it holds: +1 mood at 1 solved case, +2 at 3,
+3 at 6, +4 at 11.

## Keepsakes

Two solved cases in five leave something of the culprit's, named for it: a tooth, a ring, a claw, a locket, a key or
a hat. It says who it was taken from and where, and it's nice to look at (beauty 12).

## The first horror

The hostile monster behind a hunter's Imbuing gets a name, and the game remembers it. All three hunters
in A fresh cell share one. A monster from inside the colony, or one that wasn't hostile, leaves no horror.

Below half health it runs for the edge of the map, though you can still kill it if you trap it or burst
it down. It comes back once a season, after dark, leading a raid with more followers each time.

A part cut off it lands as its own item, named for it, at any gore setting, and doesn't rot. Study it on the
table and the colony's blows on this monster do 10% more, up to the same 25%.

Kill it, and every hunter it made fills up to their Creed's starting Conviction and gains a point of Virtue.

## Bait

Build a **bait stake** and bind a live animal or a corpse to it: right-click the body and pick **Bind to
the bait stake**. The next group of monsters on the map goes for the bait first. It takes the body when
it gets there, and then attacks as usual. If it gets hurt on the way, it forgets the bait.

Ordinary raids ignore the stake. It doesn't call anything or bring a threat sooner.

## The last stand

A hunter a monster downs, with 3 or more Conviction, burns all of it by themselves, once in a fight.

| Creed | The last stand |
|---|---|
| Avenger | Gets up and fights on for half an hour |
| Defender | Monsters nearby can attack only them for a while |
| Judge | Every monster in sight is exposed and marked, and takes 25% more damage for the rest of the fight |
| Innocent | Monsters nearby stop and stare |
| Martyr | Heals everyone nearby |
| Redeemer | The nearest monster runs or stops fighting |
| Visionary | Shows that monster's lair on the world map |

If a monster kills a hunter, one Bystander who saw it has an even chance of being imbued, whatever the
limits on new hunters say.

## Messengers' errands

About once a season a letter marks a spot on your own map, with a line like "Be at the old well
before dusk." The first hunter to go earns a point of Conviction and finds a survivor, a clue or an
ambush. Ignore it and it fades, with no other cost.

## Sparing

A Redeemer can right-click a downed monster and pick **Spare**. They help it up and it leaves unharmed. It isn't
hostile while it goes, it may come back, and the Redeemer earns the deed.
