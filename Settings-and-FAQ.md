# Settings and FAQ

## Mod settings

Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night: Imbued. Two pages: Rules, and Event chances, where each
Imbued event has its own slider from never to three times as often. **Reset to defaults** sits at
the bottom, and with a game loaded so does **Remove Imbued from this game**.

A setting you have changed shows its name tinted with a reset arrow before it, each tab counts its
changed settings, and **Changed only** by the search box lists just those.

**Rules**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Experience gained | Scales all experience a hunter earns, 25% to 300% | 100% |
| Chance of the Imbuing | How likely a witness is to wake as a hunter, before their traits, 0% to 100% | 2% |
| Hunters before the Imbuing slows | Past this many hunters in the colony, the next wakes at most once a season, 0 to 12 | 3 |
| Hunters hold True Faith | Off, a new hunter keeps only the Faith they had before | On |
| Fifth Edges from the hunter's own Creed | Off, a level 5 Edge can come from any Creed, still with the primary Virtue at 10 | On |
| Elders hide from second sight | Off, second sight sees every monster with no roll | On |

**The hunt**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Imbued among the wandering hunters | About one in four of Core's travellers and order hunters is imbued. Off, they're all ordinary people | On |
| A hunted stranger comes | A colony with no hunters gets a stranger running from a monster, between day 15 and 40 and again every 30 to 60 days until one is taken in. Off, the stranger only comes now and then as an ordinary event | On |
| Bait lures | A baited stake calls a lone monster now and then. Off, it only turns raids already on the map | On |
| The walking dead | Off, unburied corpses never rise | On |
| An exposed monster turns its town | Off, naming or exposing a monster in a town has no one take it up | On |
| Case rewards | The silver and goodwill for a solved case, 0% to 300% | 100% |
| Notes on the Hunters' Board | How often a new note goes up, 25% to 300% | 100% |

**Event chances**

Shambler raids, revenant visits, a cell under attack and a mentor's visit, each from never to three
times as often.

## FAQ

**Can I add it to a running game?** Yes. Nobody is imbued until a colonist sees a monster hurt
someone and the roll comes up.

**Can I take it out?** Use **Remove Imbued from this game** first. Every hunter becomes an ordinary
person again, the mod's things, factions and sites go, and the game is saved under a new name.

**Do I need Kindred or Garou?** No. The afflicted are the mod's own monsters. With either or both
loaded, hunters go after vampires and werewolves too.

**What about Anomaly?** Imbued ignores it. Second sight doesn't mark Anomaly's creatures.

**Can a vampire or a Garou be imbued?** No. One kind per pawn: no vampire, ghoul, Garou or
sanguophage is ever imbued.

**My hunter can't see a monster.** Check their Conviction: at 0 they're an ordinary person. A very old
or powerful monster can also be too strong for their sight.
