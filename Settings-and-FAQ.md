# Settings and FAQ

## Mod settings

Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night: Imbued. Two pages: Rules, and Event chances, where each
Imbued event has its own slider from never to three times as often. **Reset to defaults** sits at
the bottom, and with a game loaded so does **Remove Imbued from this game**.

**Rules**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Experience gained | Scales all experience a hunter earns, 25% to 300% | 100% |
| Imbued among the wandering hunters | About one in four of Core's travellers and Mendicant Order hunters is imbued. Off, they're all ordinary people | On |

**Event chances**

Shambler raids, revenant visits, a cell under attack and a mentor's visit, each from never to three
times as often.

## FAQ

**Can I add it to a running game?** Yes. Nobody is imbued until a colonist sees a monster hurt
someone and the roll comes up.

**Can I take it out?** Use **Remove Imbued from this game** first. Every hunter becomes an ordinary
person again, the mod's things, factions and sites go, and the game is saved under a new name.

**Do I need Kindred or Garou?** No. The afflicted are the mod's own monsters. With either or both
loaded, hunters go after vampires and werewolves too.

**What about Anomaly?** Imbued ignores it. Second sight doesn't mark Anomaly's creatures.

**Can a vampire or a Garou be imbued?** No. One kind per pawn: no vampire, ghoul, Garou or
sanguophage is ever imbued.

**My hunter can't see a monster.** Check their Conviction: at 0 they're an ordinary person. A very old
or powerful monster can also be too strong for their sight.
