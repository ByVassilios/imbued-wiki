# Menus and buttons

## The Imbued tab

Select a hunter and open **Imbued**. At the top are the Creed and primary Virtue, then Conviction and
Willpower as bars. Under them, four pages:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Character | The Creed, its deeds and its betrayals (hover for more); Mercy, Vision and Zeal as dots, the primary marked, with Virtue points not yet spent on an Edge ringed in gold; Willpower; Awareness, Destiny, Arsenal and Mentor |
| Attributes | The nine Attributes in three columns, Physical, Social and Mental. Hover the next dot for its cost in experience (the header shows how much there is) and click it to buy |
| Edges | Every Edge the hunter has, and **Learn an Edge...** when points are waiting |
| The hunt | Every monster this hunter knows for what it is, the colony's open files, and the habits: risking Conviction and autocast. Click a habit to change it |

At the foot: **Roll log**, the colony's last rolls and how each came out, and **Cases**, which opens
the Hunters tab on its cases.

## The Hunters tab

A button on the bottom bar once the colony has a hunter. Its three pages:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Cases | The [case window](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#the-case-window) |
| Hunters' Board | The notes and clippings on the colony's [Hunters' Board](Captured-Monsters-and-the-Board.md#the-hunters-board), once one is built. The board's own button opens this page |
| Hunter-Net | The Hunter-Net's goodwill toward the colony, and each cell the colony knows with how many hunt there. **Go to** shows the cell on the world map |

## Edge tiles

One tile on the gizmo bar for each path the hunter has an Edge on. Click it for the path's Edges,
pick one, then pick the target. An Edge that can't be used now is greyed, with the reason.

Hover an Edge to see its roll: the Attribute and Virtue with their dice, the total and the
difficulty, or the rule for it before a target is picked ("the target's Stamina + 2"). Conviction
risked shows up only in the roll itself. The roll log puts each roll's parts in a grey line under it,
and an Edge's grimoire page has the formula in a box at the top.

## Marks

While a hunter's second sight is up, every monster in sight has a mark over it. With Discern,
Witness or Illuminate the mark names what kind it is.

## Unleash

A drafted colonist has an **Unleash** button beside Fire at will. It goes after the nearest enemy
anywhere on the map and fights as an enemy of its kind would, Edges first. Anyone with a gun backs away from melee fighters and looks for cover. A pawn scared by a power stays on the map and goes back to the fight when the panic ends. Your orders come first, and once one is done it goes back to the hunt. Undrafting ends it. While it's on, a **Pick target** button chooses the enemy it goes after. Once that enemy is down or gone it picks the nearest one again, and clicking the button clears the target early.

An Edge aimed out of range or out of sight walks the hunter closer, then fires.

## Alerts

| Alert | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter can raise a Virtue | A hunter has 10 Conviction | Imbued tab, Choose a Virtue. Waiting keeps the Conviction |
| Hunter can learn an Edge | A hunter has Virtue to spare on an Edge | Imbued tab, Learn an Edge |

## Letters

| Letter | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The Imbuing | A colonist is imbued | Read it; the Messengers name the Creed |
| Warning, call for help, mentor | From [the other cells](Hunter-Net.md) | Get ready, send a caravan, or follow the lead |
