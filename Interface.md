# Menus and buttons

## The Imbued tab

Select a hunter and open **Imbued**. At the top are the Creed and primary Virtue, then Conviction and
Willpower as bars.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Creed | The Creed, its deeds and its betrayals. Hover for more |
| Virtues | Mercy, Vision and Zeal as dots, the primary marked, with unspent points under each |
| Edges | Every Edge the hunter has, and **Pick an Edge** when points are waiting |
| Background | Awareness, Destiny, Arsenal and Mentor |
| Experience | Experience to spend, the nine Attributes and Willpower. The next dot on a row shows its cost; click it to buy |
| Known monsters | Every monster this hunter knows for what it is |
| Habits | Risking Conviction and autocast. Click a row to change it |

At the foot: **Roll log**, the colony's last rolls and how each came out, and **Suspects**, the
[suspect list](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#the-suspect-list).

## Edge tiles

One tile on the gizmo bar for each path the hunter has an Edge on. Click it for the path's Edges,
pick one, then pick the target. An Edge that can't be used now is greyed, with the reason.

## Marks

While a hunter's second sight is up, every monster in sight has a mark over it. With Discern,
Witness or Illuminate the mark names what kind it is.

## Unleash

A drafted colonist has an **Unleash** button beside Fire at will. It goes after the nearest enemy
anywhere on the map and fights as an enemy of its kind would, Edges first. Your orders come first, and once one is done it goes back to the hunt. Undrafting ends it.

## Letters

| Letter | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The Imbuing | A colonist is imbued | Read it; the Messengers name the Creed |
| A Virtue point | A hunter reaches 10 Conviction | Pick the Virtue to raise, or keep the Conviction for now |
| A new Edge | Virtue points open an Edge | Pick it, or put it off |
| Warning, call for help, mentor | From [the Courier Union](Hunter-Net.md) | Get ready, send a caravan, or follow the lead |
