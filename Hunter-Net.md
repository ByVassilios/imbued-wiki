# The other cells

Hunter cells trade as an ordinary-looking faction whose name, color, people and post labels change every game, so its name tells you nothing.

Other cells of hunters, scattered across the world. They stay hidden until one reaches out: 3 to 8
days after the colony's first hunter, the nearest cell sends a letter naming only itself, and the
cells are allies from then on.

The other cells stay off the world map one by one until you learn of them. A letter or call for help
from a cell shows it, a raid shows where it came from, your hunters can meet its people, and a cell
you know will now and then vouch for another.

Hunters don't get a list of monsters either. A town's inspect line says who lives there only after a
hunter has seen them for what they are on that map: "Found out here so far", as a share of the town,
counting each person once.

## Cells

Four cell settlements, two of them within reach of the colony. Their people are cell members
(Bystanders with guns) and hunters with a Creed. Each cell sells the Core hunter weapons (stake
crossbow, fire lance, aspergillum, incendiary shotgun) with plain supplies, and buys anything of
value. A hunter with [Arsenal](Character-Sheet.md#backgrounds) gets better prices.

## When a cell falls

Cells hunt the courts and septs near them, and get hunted back. A cell's hunters have names and outlive
its fall: some die, some go missing, and the rest go to the nearest cell or start a new one. While
the network is short of its cells, new ones are found about twice a year.

- **Exposure.** Each cell has a rating, shown in words once you know it. Every hunt it makes and every
  witness who gets away raises it; lying low lowers it. A more exposed cell is struck more often.
- **Sieges.** When a war is about to break a cell you know, it is under siege for a few days first, and
  a quest lets you go and help.
- **Letters.** A cell you know falling gets a letter, and taking one yourself says so. The other cells
  traffic brings rumors of the rest.
- **Refugees.** Hunters who like the colony and are within reach may ask to join. Your hunters gain
  Conviction against whatever kind of monster did it.

Core's settings, under The world of darkness, have a switch for all of this (off: a fallen home's people just vanish), a bloodiness slider for falls with no battle, and a world pace slider.

## Standing

The colony's standing with hunter-net is its goodwill with the cells. Each solved
[case](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#solving-a-case) raises it by 10 to 25, more for a harder case.

| Standing | What it opens |
|---|---|
| 20 | Tips: every four days or so an open case gets a letter from a cell that knows the town. It tells what kind of monster did it, or once that's known, rules out an innocent suspect |
| 40 | A hunter-net trader caravan comes by now and then, selling the arsenal, medicine and chemfuel |
| 60 | **Call a cell** in the case window: two hunters come to the case's town or lair, once every ten days |
| 80 | Rumours come from 8 tiles farther, and cases pay a quarter more |

## Warnings

One time in three, a cell sends word of an afflicted raid or a revenant's visit before it comes:
what's coming, and in 6 to 12 hours.

## Calls for help

Now and then a cell 2 to 8 tiles off is attacked by the dead and asks the colony to come. The call is a
quest: take it within a day or two, and the cell's three hunters hold for three days after. Arrive in
time and keep one alive, and the cell pays 300 silver and the net thinks better of you.

## Asking for help

The other cells are an ally, so the comms console's call for military aid reaches them, and a few hunters
come to fight beside the colony.
Without a console, pin **Ask for help** on [the hunters' board](Captured-Monsters-and-the-Board.md#your-own-notes).

## Mentors

A hunter with the Mentor Background is visited now and then by an older hunter, more often for more
dots. The mentor brings either the weakness of a monster kind the colony hasn't pinpointed (from then
on, the colony's hunters hit that kind a quarter harder), or the lair of a monster one of the colony's
hunters knows.

## Hunters in other bands

About one in four of Core's wandering travellers and order hunters is imbued, mostly
Avengers, Defenders and Judges, with two or three Edges. They see through a vampire's mask without a
roll, and a mendicant hunter goes straight for a masked vampire. They use their Edges in a fight. A
setting turns them off.
