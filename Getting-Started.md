# Getting started

## Install

You need RimWorld 1.6, Harmony and The Rim by Night: Core. Load order: Harmony, Core, then Imbued.
No DLC is needed. Kindred and Garou can load alongside it, in any combination; see
[Living with monsters](Living-with-Monsters.md).

The mod is safe to add to a running save. Your colonists stay ordinary until one of them sees a
monster hurt someone and the Messengers choose them.

## Scenarios

Three scenarios start you with hunters.

| Scenario | Who you start with |
|---|---|
| Fresh cell | Three new hunters, each with the Imbuing's three Virtue points and one Edge |
| Lone survivor | One new hunter. A shambler raid comes about two and a half days in |
| Hunting party | Four veterans with six to eight Virtue points, two or three Edges each and hunter weapons, and a revenant's lair on the world map |

Each colonist in these scenarios starts with one random weapon in hand, picked from the scenario's own list. Some scenarios add a steel helmet, a robe or both. Arsenal packs an extra hunter weapon a dot on top.

To make your own, add the "starting hunters" part in the scenario editor. It sets how many of the
first colonists are hunters.

## Getting imbued in play

Any adult colonist who sees a monster hurt someone within about 15 cells rolls for the Imbuing.
The chance is small (2%, more for the brave and the kind), and the colony gets at most one a day
from the same monster. What the colonist does in the next few seconds picks their Virtue: fighting
the monster leans to Zeal, helping the hurt to Mercy, anything else to Vision. Someone who just
misses the roll becomes a Bystander. See [Being imbued](Being-Imbued.md).

Without Kindred or Garou, the monsters that set this off are the afflicted. See
[The afflicted](The-Walking-Dead.md).

## A first-night checklist

1. Open the hunter's **Imbued** tab: Creed, Virtues, the first Edge, and the Background.
2. Set the hunter's risk policy and autocast in the tab's Habits rows.
3. Keep fire close: torches, a campfire, incendiaries. The afflicted burn.
4. Bury your dead. Corpses left lying out can get up when the map is haunted.
